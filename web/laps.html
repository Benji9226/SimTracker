How to read this page
  • Clean lap: a full lap that didn't go through the pit lane and that the game counted. Only clean laps can be saved, or count as a best lap. Other full laps (out laps, pit laps, track limits) are listed in grey and can still be compared.
  • Time gap (top chart): when the line goes up, the lap you're checking is falling behind the other lap. When it goes down, it's catching up.
  • Brake and throttle: if one lap brakes earlier, or gets back on the throttle later, you'll see it here.
  • T1, T2 ... along the top are the turns, so you can see which part of the track you're looking at.

Scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click to zoom out. "Room to the track edge" colours each line by how far the car was from the edge of the track (the game measures this). The grey track surface is worked out from where the game says the edges are in every lap you have driven on this track. It is remembered between sessions, so it gets more accurate the more you drive here; where no lap went near an edge it is filled in from the places that did.
