# Track map credits

The track outlines in this folder were redrawn (simplified and recoloured) from free maps on Wikimedia Commons.
Maps under *CC BY-SA* are shared here under the same licence, with credit to their authors. The outlines are derived works.
The app never goes online: these are the only copies it uses.
