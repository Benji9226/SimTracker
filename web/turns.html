What do these numbers mean?
  • Your best: the fastest you've ever been through the turn on a clean lap.
  • Typical: your average time through the turn.
  • Lost per lap: typical minus best. This is time you can already do, but don't do every lap. It's the safest place to start.
  • Saved laps: your personal best (and laps you saved) from other days count as extra tries at every turn. Turn the tick box off to see this session only.
  • Ideal lap: your best time through every turn, added together. You've shown you can do each part, just not all in one lap.
  • Coasting: seconds in the turn with neither throttle nor brake pressed. Some is normal. A lot usually means getting back on the power late.
  • Wait for full throttle: how long after the slowest point you take to reach full throttle. Blank in the first half of a chicane.
