How these numbers are measured
  • Only clean laps count: full laps of a normal length and time, so out laps, pit visits and spins are left out.
  • TC per lap: seconds per lap that traction control was active. ABS braking: the share of the time your brake pedal was pressed that ABS was active.
  • Inner minus outer edge: how much hotter the inner edge of the tyre runs than the outer edge. More negative camber raises it.
  • "Same places" means in at least 60% of the clean laps the effect came on in the same 100 m stretch of track.
  • Lap times vary by about half a second from run to run for reasons that have nothing to do with the setup, so the other numbers are the better guide.
